DEC MicroVAX 3900 — KA655 — OpenVMS VAX

Pick a ka655x.bin ROM and an ODS-2 .dsk image, then press Start.

The disk is read lazily, 64 KB at a time, and is never modified: writes land in an
in-memory copy-on-write overlay.  Click this screen to give it the keyboard.

A boot to the OpenVMS login prompt is a few hundred million instructions and takes
a couple of minutes; output streams as it goes.

Click the screen to type. Backspace sends DEL (VMS rubout); Ctrl-key combinations are sent as control characters.
